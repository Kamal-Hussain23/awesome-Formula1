### Getting Started

* [Formula 1 Official Website](https://www.formula1.com/) - The authoritative source for race schedules, standings, news, regulations, and official F1 content.
* [F1 TV](https://f1tv.formula1.com/) - Offers live race coverage, onboard cameras, team radio, replays, and extensive historical archives.
* [Motorsport.com F1](https://www.motorsport.com/f1/) - Provides comprehensive Formula 1 news, analysis, interviews, and technical coverage from a global motorsport newsroom.
* [Autosport F1](https://www.autosport.com/f1/) - Delivers in-depth reporting, expert commentary, and historical context on Formula 1 developments.
* [Wikipedia: Formula One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One) - A useful reference for understanding the sport’s history, teams, drivers, regulations, and records.

### Advanced Tools & Analysis

* [StatsF1](https://www.statsf1.com/) - A detailed statistical database covering Formula 1 results, drivers, teams, qualifying sessions, and historical records.
* [RacingNews365](https://racingnews365.com/) - Features timely F1 news, race analysis, podcasts, and insights into championship developments.
* [The Race F1](https://www.the-race.com/formula-1/) - Offers expert analysis, technical breakdowns, and opinion pieces from experienced Formula 1 journalists.
* [FIA](https://www.fia.com/) - Publishes official sporting and technical regulations, steward decisions, and governance documents for Formula 1.
* [RaceFans](https://www.racefans.net/) - Combines independent journalism, live race coverage, data resources, and thoughtful discussion of Formula 1 issues.
